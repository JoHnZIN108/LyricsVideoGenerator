# Illustration prompts

Generated art for the Illustration scene, thumbnails and B-roll. The art carries the concept; all words are live type added on top.

## Rules

1. **Flat vector.** Simple geometric shapes, clean edges, isometric or straight-on views. No photorealism, no 3D render look, no painterly texture.
2. **Dark navy ground** (#0a1128, fading to #050918 at the edges). Surfaces #111b3d / #18265a.
3. **Two neon inks only**: electric orange #ff7a1a and cyan #22d3ee, as 4–7px outlines and small fills. Cyan = the AI / the answer; orange = the human side, warnings, sparks of attention. No other hues.
4. **Glow is light, not colour**: a soft bloom around the ONE key object; everything else stays flat.
5. **Composition**: subject in the right 55% of a 16:9 frame; the left 45% is calm, dark and empty for the headline. Leave room for 2–3 labels near the parts that matter.
6. **Blueprint touches allowed**: faint grid, dashed guide circles, dimension ticks — at low opacity.
7. **No text anywhere in the image**: no letters, numbers, UI copy, logos or watermarks. Screens and pages show grey bars instead of words.
8. **No faces, no mascots, no brand logos or real products.** People (if needed) are faceless flat silhouettes.

## Prompt template

> Flat vector illustration, 16:9, of **[SUBJECT — concrete objects that show the idea]**. Deep navy background (#0a1128) with a subtle blueprint grid and soft radial vignette. Objects built from simple geometric shapes with clean neon outlines in cyan (#22d3ee) and electric orange (#ff7a1a), dark navy fills, a soft glow around **[KEY OBJECT]** only. Subject placed in the right half, left 45% of the frame empty and dark for a headline. Minimal, clean, modern explainer style. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no faces, no gradients except the glow.

**Negative:** text, letters, words, typography, watermark, logo, photorealistic, 3D render, faces, characters, busy background, extra colours, purple, green, lens flare.

## Examples

- *Training*: "stacks of books and floating browser windows streaming small glowing squares along dotted paths into a single glowing cyan cube" (key object: the cube).
- *Next-word prediction*: "a glowing cyan die mid-roll above a row of blank rounded tiles, one tile outlined in orange" (key object: the die).
- *Hallucination*: "a confident cyan speech bubble whose tail turns into a tangle of orange wire" (key object: the speech bubble).
